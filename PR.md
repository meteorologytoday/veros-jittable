# PR: NaN-safe AD through jax.jvp + NaN/Inf state debugging infrastructure

**Branch:** `dev/naive-fix-AD-needs-physical-justification` → `main`

## Summary

Differentiating chained Veros model steps with `jax.jvp` was producing NaN sensitivity tangents. This PR fixes the two root causes and adds infrastructure (a NaN/Inf state scanner, gated by a setting) that was used to track them down and that stays useful for future AD debugging.

⚠️ **Needs physical review before merge** — see "Open question" below. The numerical fix is sound as a differentiability patch, but one branch changes how a negative `Nsqr` (Brunt-Väisälä frequency squared) is treated, which has physical meaning (convective instability), not just numerical meaning.

## Changes

### 1. AD-safe sqrt near/at zero and negative inputs
- Added `utilities.sqrt_singularity_removed(x, eps=1e-12)`, replacing `sqrt(maximum(0, x))` and the Huber-shift `sqrt(x + eps)` pattern at call sites in `eke.py`, `idemix.py`, and `tke.py`.
- `sqrt(maximum(0, x))` has a kink at `x=0` (infinite/NaN tangent under JAX's `maximum` JVP rule at the tie point); `sqrt(x + eps)` is unsafe once `x` can be genuinely negative (e.g. `Nsqr` in convectively unstable regions, or `tke`/`eke` with small numerical undershoot from the implicit solver) since it evaluates `sqrt` of a negative number.
- `sqrt(maximum(x, eps))` keeps the argument `>= eps > 0` for any sign of `x`, so both the primal and its derivative (`1/(2*sqrt(maximum(x,eps)))`) stay finite everywhere.

### 2. Clamp sub-surface TKE to non-negative
- `integrate_tke_kernel` (`tke.py`) previously only floored the **surface** level at `>= 0` (tracking the deficit via `tke_surf_corr`). Sub-surface levels could carry small numerical-undershoot values (~`-1e-7`, against typical TKE magnitudes of ~`1e-4`) out of the implicit tridiagonal solve.
- Extended the same non-negative floor to all sub-surface levels. Negligible effect on the forward solution, but prevents those small negatives from reaching `sqrt_singularity_removed` on the next step and producing NaN tangents.

### 3. NaN/Inf state scanner (`veros/debug_tools.py`)
- New `detect_nan_in_state(state, header=...)`: scans every float field in `state.variables` for NaN/Inf and reports via `jax.debug.print` (works both eagerly and while traced under `jit`/`grad`/`jvp`).
- Wired into checkpoints throughout `VerosSetup.step` (`veros.py`) — after forcing, each parameterization update, momentum, thermodynamics, advection, boundary exchange, etc. — to narrow down where non-finite values first appear in a run.
- Gated by a new `enable_nan_checks` setting (`settings.py`), off by default.

### 4. `affects_tracing` flag on `Setting` (`settings.py`, `state.py`)
- `Setting` gained an `affects_tracing` field (default `True`). `enable_nan_checks` and other I/O-only settings (`identifier`, `description`, `restart_*`) are marked `affects_tracing=False`.
- `veros_state_pytree_flatten` (`state.py`) now hashes only `affects_tracing=True` settings into the JIT pseudo-hash, so toggling `enable_nan_checks` (or renaming a restart file, etc.) doesn't trigger a spurious retrace of the whole step function. This is also what lets `enable_nan_checks=False` remove the check ops from the jaxpr entirely rather than just branching around them at runtime.

### 5. Disabled `isoneutral.check_isoneutral_slope_crit`
- Commented out the call in `VerosSetup.setup()` (`veros.py`) — it was breaking the model. Left as a comment rather than deleted pending a real fix or a decision to remove it for good.

### 6. Misc
- `solve_pressure.py`: added a missing import needed for the above to run.
- `CLAUDE.md`: added project instructions for working in this repo with Claude Code.

## Open question / why this needs a second pair of eyes

`sqrt_singularity_removed` treats `Nsqr < 0` by clamping the *argument to `sqrt`* at `eps`, purely to keep AD finite. `Nsqr < 0` is not a numerical error, though — it's the physical signature of a convectively unstable water column, and downstream quantities derived from `sqrt(Nsqr)` (Rossby radius, buoyancy length scale, IDEMIX forcing) previously went through `sqrt(maximum(0, Nsqr))`, i.e. the same clamp-to-zero-ish behavior in the primal, so forward-model numerics are unchanged. What changes is the *sensitivity*: gradients/tangents that would have been generated by the convective instability itself are now floored to zero at that point, since the clamp is active. Someone with more physical-oceanography context should confirm that zeroing the AD sensitivity through the convective-instability branch is the right call (vs., e.g., a smooth `sqrt`-relaxation of `Nsqr` itself that preserves some gradient signal), rather than just accepting it because it happens to make `jax.jvp` return finite numbers. Hence the branch name.

## Testing

- Ran `jax.jvp` through a chained multi-step Veros integration; tangents are finite where they previously came out NaN.
- `enable_nan_checks=True` used during development to localize both root causes to `sqrt_singularity_removed` and `integrate_tke_kernel`; scanner left in place (default off) for future use.
